How the proposed system works

01 / PARTICIPATE

Use the mining app

Players use the official KINGMAKER mining application and complete the activity types that will be published in the final rules.

02 / CROWN REWARDS

Earn $CROWN

Eligible mining activity is validated by the service. Approved rewards are recorded in a transparent CROWN ledger.

03 / KGM ELIGIBILITY

Separate $KGM rewards

Eligible miners may separately qualify for KGM distributions from the published ecosystem rewards allocation. Eligibility and timing will be defined before launch.

Two assets. Different purposes.

$CROWN

Mining reward currency

Earned through eligible activity in the KINGMAKER mining application. It is not a loan, borrowing product or guaranteed cash equivalent.

$KGM

Governance & utility token

Proposed token with separately governed eligibility and distribution rules. A CROWN balance does not automatically convert into KGM.
