01 — The KINGMAKER vision

KINGMAKER is a web-first civilization strategy experience. Players develop nations, manage resources, build infrastructure, research technologies, trade, respond to world events and shape a persistent-feeling 3D world. The long-term ambition is an intelligent, creator-aware ecosystem where meaningful participation can be recognized transparently.

02 — Two currencies, two different jobs

$CROWN

Mining reward currency

The in-game reward currency credited to players for eligible mining and verified participation. CROWN records rewards earned through the KINGMAKER mining application. CROWN has no promised cash value.

$KGM

Governance & utility token

Proposed external blockchain token for eligible governance, licensing marketplace functions, staking, access/entry and collateral use cases—only where legally permitted and technically supported.

Core separation: CROWN is the mining-app reward currency. KGM is the proposed governance and utility token. Mining activity can earn CROWN; it does not trigger an immediate KGM distribution. There is no automatic fixed CROWN-to-KGM conversion.

03 — Participation & the proposed mining model

“Mining” in KINGMAKER is a proposed participation-and-contribution programme, not hidden CPU/GPU cryptocurrency mining. The intended experience is straightforward: players use the mining application, complete eligible participation activity and receive CROWN rewards according to transparent rules. Mining must be visible, consent-based and validated by the service; it must not secretly use device processing power.

Launch sequence: First, complete and launch the KINGMAKER game. Next, establish the ecosystem and complete the token, compliance, security and distribution preparations. Only after those milestones may the project launch KGM and distribute it under separately published eligibility and allocation rules. Eligible miners may be considered for a distribution from the designated pool, but mining itself does not automatically issue KGM. KGM distribution is a later ecosystem milestone—not an instant mining reward. It remains separate from the CROWN ledger and has no fixed KGM-per-CROWN rate, guaranteed reward, yield or monetary value.

  • No background device mining or battery-draining compute.
  • No payment required to participate in basic gameplay.
  • Activity scoring must be server-validated; browser-only counters are demos, not proof.
  • Anti-bot, multi-account, collusion and exploit controls are required before rewards.
  • Reward budgets and emission limits must be public and auditable.

05 — Proposed utility

  • DAO governance: vote on eligible ecosystem proposals under a legally reviewed governance framework.
  • Licensing marketplace: access or settle eligible creator licensing functions.
  • Staking: potential network participation mechanism; no APY or yield is promised.
  • Entry: access to selected events or premium ecosystem functions where permitted.
  • Collateral: optional collateral utility only after risk controls, liquidation rules and legal review.

06 — Intelligent world architecture

The roadmap targets an evolving simulation with rule-governed world systems, increasingly capable AI agents, dynamic events, technology progression and player-created content. AI features must be bounded by deterministic game rules, player controls, abuse monitoring and clear disclosure. The current prototype uses local simulation logic; advanced AI orchestration is future development.

07 — Roadmap

  1. Foundation: playable web prototype, stability, saves, controls and automated testing.
  2. Living world: higher-fidelity 3D environments, world activity, performance and scale.
  3. Intelligence: richer agents, dynamic events and responsive civilization systems.
  4. Economy: define CROWN reward rules, eligible activity, game utility and contribution scoring.
  5. Web3 design: finalize KGM token utility, chain selection, custody assumptions and governance.
  6. Game launch: complete, test and launch the KINGMAKER game before any KGM distribution.
  7. Ecosystem establishment: establish the network, publish final eligibility and distribution rules, and complete legal, technical and security reviews.
  8. KGM launch & distribution: launch the token only after the prior gates; distribute from the approved allocation to eligible participants, including eligible miners where the published rules provide.

08 — Security, compliance & player protection

Before any real token or reward launch, KINGMAKER requires jurisdiction-specific legal advice, risk disclosures, privacy and consumer-protection review, independent smart-contract audits, secure key management, incident response, transparent treasury governance and tested withdrawal/claim procedures. Wallet connection must never request seed phrases or private keys. A connected address alone does not prove a unique human or eligibility.

09 — Risks & disclaimers

Digital assets can lose all value. Tokens may be restricted or unavailable in some jurisdictions. Game balances may be changed for balancing, fraud prevention or operational reasons. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Staking, collateral, liquidity and marketplace functions carry technical, market, counterparty and regulatory risks. No financial return, listing, liquidity, conversion rate or launch date is guaranteed.

10 — Launch order & current status

  1. Build, test and launch the KINGMAKER game.
  2. Establish the ecosystem and finalize token, eligibility, compliance and security preparations.
  3. Launch $KGM and distribute it under the published allocation and eligibility rules.

Mining rewards are a separate track: the mining application is intended to award CROWN for eligible verified activity. KGM is not automatically issued when someone mines. Eligible miners may separately qualify for a later KGM distribution after the game launch and ecosystem-establishment milestones. This paper records the intended design as of November 2026. The playable game and network portal are prototypes. CROWN, KGM, mining, conversion, DAO voting, staking, licensing settlement, collateral and payouts are not live. Future changes will be published with version history before implementation.
