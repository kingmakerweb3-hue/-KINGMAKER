AUTHOR YOUR WORLD

Creator Studio

Publish a design, then commission it for 30 credits and 12 materials. Up to three run nationally. Rival nations discover and use published designs when their real needs match the category; successful first use earns a bounded royalty and creator progression.

Royalty: 5 + floor(world demand ÷ 20) credits on first successful use by each rival, at most once per design per rival. Each success adds 10 experience and 5 reputation; every 50 experience raises the creator level. No external marketplace, real-money value, or token integration.
