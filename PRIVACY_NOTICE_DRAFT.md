# KINGMAKER privacy notice — draft

**Status: Draft for publisher review. Do not treat this as legal advice or publish unchanged without confirming the actual hosting, analytics, contact and data practices.**

## Current prototype behavior
The current browser prototype stores game progress in the browser's local storage on the device used to play. The game provides JSON export/import for local save backups. Local saves are not automatically synchronized between devices.

The prototype does not currently provide game accounts, cloud saves, live multiplayer, or in-game payment processing.

## Hosting and server logs
When the game is hosted publicly, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information, requested files, timestamps and security logs. The publisher must confirm the actual host's practices and retention periods and add accurate details here before launch.

## Analytics, cookies and advertising
Do not claim that analytics, advertising cookies or tracking are absent or present until the deployed site has been inspected and the publisher has documented the actual configuration. Add details and any required consent mechanism before enabling these services.

## Purchases
The current prototype does not process purchases. If paid features are added, this notice must identify the payment provider, the information it receives, the purpose of processing, refund/support route and applicable retention practices.

## Children's privacy
Before launch, the publisher must decide the intended audience and assess applicable child privacy and consumer protection requirements for every territory in which the game is offered.

## Contact and publisher details
Add the publisher's legal/business name, a monitored privacy contact address, and an effective date before publication.

## User choices
Players can export their local save. Clearing browser storage may erase local progress, so players should keep a backup. Add instructions for any additional data rights or deletion process if accounts, analytics or other personal data processing are introduced.
